# References

The conventions and derivations used in the theory pages follow standard nonequilibrium field-theory literature and the open-system extensions used by this package:

- A. Kamenev, *Field Theory of Non-Equilibrium Systems*, Cambridge University Press (2011).
- L. M. Sieberer *et al.*, [Keldysh Field Theory for Driven Open Quantum Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00637).
- F. Thompson *et al.*, [Field theory of many-body Lindbladian dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aop.2023.169385).
- F. Gerbino *et al.*, [Kinetics of Quantum Reaction-Diffusion systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.20028).
- The symbolic field-algebra conventions used by KeldyshContraction.jl are aligned with [SciPost Phys. Core **8**, 014 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.21468/SciPostPhysCore.8.1.014).
