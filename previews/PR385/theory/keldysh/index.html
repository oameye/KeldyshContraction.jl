Schwinger–Keldysh field theory

KeldyshContraction.jl works with bosonic and fermionic fields on the closed real-time contour. The contour doubles every physical field into a forward and backward branch,

\[\psi_+(x),\qquad \psi_-(x),\]

so that real-time expectation values are generated by a single contour action. Hamiltonian terms enter as the forward action minus the backward action; Markovian jump processes add contour-coupled dissipative vertices. Equal-time ordering of those vertices is discussed separately in Equal-time regularisation.

Bosons

For bosons the package uses the classical/quantum rotation

\[\psi_c=\frac{\psi_++\psi_-}{\sqrt2},\qquad \psi_q=\frac{\psi_+-\psi_-}{\sqrt2},\]

with the same rotation for barred fields. The Green function and self-energy are stored semantically as retarded, advanced, and Keldysh components and assembled as

\[\hat G_B= \begin{pmatrix} G^K&G^R\\ G^A&0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \hat\Sigma_B= \begin{pmatrix} 0&\Sigma^A\\ \Sigma^R&\Sigma^K \end{pmatrix}.\]

The public labels Classical and Quantum denote the two Keldysh components.

Fermions

For fermions the package uses the asymmetric Larkin–Ovchinnikov rotation

\[\psi_1=\frac{\psi_++\psi_-}{\sqrt2},\qquad \psi_2=\frac{\psi_+-\psi_-}{\sqrt2},\]

\[\bar\psi_1=\frac{\bar\psi_+-\bar\psi_-}{\sqrt2},\qquad \bar\psi_2=\frac{\bar\psi_++\bar\psi_-}{\sqrt2}.\]

With unbarred index on the row and barred index on the column,

\[\hat G_F= \begin{pmatrix} G^R&G^K\\ 0&G^A \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \hat\Sigma_F= \begin{pmatrix} \Sigma^R&\Sigma^K\\ 0&\Sigma^A \end{pmatrix}.\]

The public labels One and Two denote these components. For fermions bar(ψ) is the independent barred Grassmann variable, not an operator adjoint. Canonical products obey Grassmann antisymmetry; Wick-contraction signs are generated by the permutation parity of the fermionic pairing.

Spectral and statistical components

The kinetic convention is statistics independent at the level of retarded and advanced functions,

\[A=i\left(G^R-G^A\right).\]

The exact Keldysh parametrisation is

\[G^K=G^R\circ F-F\circ G^A,\]

where \circ denotes the space-time convolution, or the corresponding Moyal product after Wigner transformation. In the homogeneous zeroth-gradient limit,

\[G^K=-iFA.\]

Statistics enters through the relation between F and the occupation,

\[F_B=1+2n_B, \qquad F_F=1-2n_F.\]

This separation is central to the package: the diagrammatic and kinetic pipeline is shared by bosons and fermions, while statistics controls field algebra, Wick signs, Keldysh conventions, and the final occupation map.

What the package represents

A physical field family is declared once and then carries statistics, Keldysh component, barred/unbarred orientation, position, equal-time regularisation, and coordinate derivatives as explicit symbolic data. The same representation is used from the interaction action through Wick contraction, self-energy extraction, Fourier and Wigner transformation, and kinetic reduction.

The next page, Perturbation theory and self-consistency, explains how the contour action is converted into the 1PI self-energy used by the kinetic theory.
