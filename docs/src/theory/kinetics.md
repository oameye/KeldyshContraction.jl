# Quantum kinetic theory

The package lowers a Wigner-space self-energy to a collision kernel in explicit stages. Each stage corresponds to a distinct physical approximation or algebraic reduction.

## Wigner representation

For a two-point function `G(x_1,x_2)`, introduce centre and relative coordinates,

```math
X=\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},
\qquad
s=x_1-x_2,
```

and Fourier transform in `s`. Convolutions become Moyal products. The current kinetic compiler uses the homogeneous zeroth-gradient limit, where the Moyal product reduces to ordinary multiplication.

## Kadanoff--Baym collision identity

With

```math
A_\Sigma=i\left(\Sigma^R-\Sigma^A\right),
```

the package uses the off-shell collision combination

```math
I_{\mathrm{coll}}
=i\Sigma^K-iF_k\left(\Sigma^R-\Sigma^A\right)
=i\Sigma^K-F_kA_\Sigma.
```

This identity is formed from the **complete** retarded and advanced self-energies. No product of retarded/advanced lines is replaced by a product of individual imaginary parts.

For the internal propagators,

```math
G^K=-iFA
```

at zeroth gradient order, with

```math
F_B=1+2n_B,
\qquad
F_F=1-2n_F.
```

Bosons and fermions therefore share the same collision-reduction algorithm; statistics changes the occupation map and the diagrammatic signs upstream.

## Spectral and dispersive parts

A causal propagator contains both spectral and dispersive information. Frequency reduction separates finite contributions into energy-shell and principal-value support,

```math
\delta(\Delta E),
\qquad
\operatorname{PV}\!\left(\frac{1}{\Delta E}\right).
```

The shell terms generate the usual on-shell gain/loss structure. Principal-value terms retain virtual, dispersive contributions and must not be discarded merely because they are off shell.

Momentum conservation is treated exactly and loop momenta are quotiented only after the statistical polynomial has been reduced. This gives a canonical representation of physically equivalent collision terms.

## Strict quasiparticle limit

The final finite kernel has the schematic form

```math
C[n](k)
=\int_{\boldsymbol q\ldots}
\mathcal K(k,\boldsymbol q,\ldots)
\,\mathcal N[n]
\,\delta(\Delta E)
```

with additional principal-value sectors when present. `\mathcal K` contains the exact momentum polynomial generated by derivative vertices and `\mathcal N[n]` is the bosonic or fermionic occupation polynomial.

Not every causal frequency structure has a finite strict-quasiparticle value. Degenerate retarded/advanced poles can produce a genuine pinch singularity. KeldyshContraction.jl keeps such contributions explicit as typed blockers; it does **not** assign them an arbitrary finite coefficient. A finite-width or resummed treatment is a separate approximation layer.

## Compiler chain

The user-facing sequence is

```text
Σᴿ,Σᴬ,Σᴷ
  ↓ kinetic_expression
spectral/statistical self-energy
  ↓ off_shell_collision_expression
Kadanoff--Baym collision expression
  ↓ spectral_dispersive_collision
spectral/dispersive basis
  ↓ reduce_frequency_collision
shell / PV / blocked frequency support
  ↓ occupation_reduced_expression
occupation polynomial
  ↓ quotient_loop_momenta
canonical loop representation
  ↓ collision_kernel
C[n]
```

The [Kinetic reduction](../manual/kinetics.md) manual page documents the corresponding public functions.
